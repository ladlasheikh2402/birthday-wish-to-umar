Bhai ke naam ek khat

Bhai, kuch rishte lafzon ke mohtaj nahi hote, aur hamara rishta unmein se ek hai. Bachpan se le kar aaj tak, har mushkil waqt mein jo sahara aap ne diya, wo kabhi bhulaya nahi ja sakta.

Aap sirf bade bhai nahi, balke wo saya hain jiske neeche hum hamesha mehfooz mehsoos karte hain. Aap ki mehnat, aap ka sabar aur aap ka pyaar humein har din kuch naya sikhata hai.

Is naye saal mein Allah aap ko wo tamam khushiyan de jo aap dusron ko dete hain, aap ki har dua qubool ho aur aap hamesha aise hi muskurate rahein.
